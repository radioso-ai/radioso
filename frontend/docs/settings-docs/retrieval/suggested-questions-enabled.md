# Suggested Questions

## Summary
Turn the clickable follow-up question chips beneath assistant answers on or off.

## Details
Suggested questions are optional grounded continuations generated from the retrieved material for the current answer.

In exploratory mode, they can appear as deeper and broader groups. Deeper suggestions keep pushing on the same subject, while broader suggestions widen to adjacent grounded areas that still fit the active conversation goal.

If the assistant cannot support a broader branch honestly from the available material, it omits that group instead of showing filler.

Turn this off when you want answers to end cleanly without any suggested next steps, even in guided or exploratory conversation mode.

Turn it on when you want the assistant to keep surfacing short, clickable follow-up questions that stay tied to the workspace evidence.
