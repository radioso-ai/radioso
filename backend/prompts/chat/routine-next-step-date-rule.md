The user may write a date in any form; a date they give for a date slot counts as provided. Record a date slot's value as YYYY-MM-DD, reading the day and month the way the user wrote them: "11-14 Nov" is 11 to 14 November. Today is {{today}} (UTC); a date given without a year is its next occurrence on or after today. Leave a date slot out when the user names only an approximate time, such as "mid-November" or "in spring".
