# Content planning

Open **Activity → Content plan** when you want to decide what knowledge work to do
next. The page combines the last 30 days of visitor questions with grounding and corpus
evidence, then keeps that view updated as conversations are committed.

There is no report to generate. New eligible turns join the durable projection in the
background, and the header tells you how current the visible evidence is.

## Start with Recommended next

When the evidence supports an action, **Recommended next** shows one topic rather than
a wall of equally weighted cards. It explains why the topic ranks first, shows the
measured answer evidence, and gives you the relevant next step.

The action has a specific meaning:

- **Add content** — no sufficiently related document was found.
- **Investigate retrieval** — relevant knowledge existed, but affected answers usually
  failed to retrieve or cite it.
- **Review existing content** — related knowledge was retrieved but insufficient, or a
  relevant document changed after the gap appeared.
- **Monitor** — visitors are interested, but the evidence does not justify remediation.

If the corpus check cannot run, Radioso leaves the action unavailable. It does not turn
missing evidence into an “Add content” recommendation.

## Read demand and grounding separately

The summary separates visitor demand from answer quality. **Visitor questions** and
**Topics** describe interest. **Opportunities** counts mature topics with active
reduced/no-support evidence from at least two conversations.

Grounding uses three measured verdicts: grounded, reduced support, and no support.
Answers without a verdict appear separately as **not evaluated**, because missing
measurement is not a fourth failure category. For small samples the page emphasizes raw
counts instead of making a percentage look conclusive.

Switch from **Content opportunities** to **All interests** to include well-covered
topics. Questions that have not yet formed a mature topic stay in the quieter
**Emerging questions** section.

## Inspect the evidence

Select a topic to keep the ranked list in view on desktop and open its full detail on a
narrow screen. The URL is shareable. Topic detail puts the decision and its evidence
before generated prose, then shows:

- questions the content should answer
- representative visitor questions
- possibly related documents and what affected answers retrieved or cited
- affected agents and channels

Use **View answers** to open the existing Quality queue filtered to this topic. Use a
related document to inspect the existing source in Knowledge. **Write document** opens
the ordinary inline document flow with only a suggested title and question outline;
you still review and write every factual claim.

<Callout type="warning" emoji="i">
  Generated labels and briefs organize the evidence. They do not control ranking, and
  they are not factual source material. Verify facts against your approved documents
  before publishing anything.
</Callout>

## Understand freshness

The `as of` timestamp identifies the coherent report snapshot. When background work is
still running, a quiet status strip separates pending embeddings, topic assignments,
and enrichments. During bootstrap, delay, or embedding-space reprojection, the last
coherent evidence remains visible when available.

Provider trouble can leave a label or brief pending, stale, or unavailable without
hiding the topic's demand and grounding counts.

## What does not become a topic

Content Planning observes semantic information needs, not every message. Greetings,
button-like choices, “yes,” “go ahead,” and routine field values do not become topics by
themselves. Contextual follow-ups and clarification replies are tied back to the
question they complete. An unresolved fragment expires rather than becoming a
misleading cluster.

Source wording remains on the original conversation message. Privacy deletion removes
that wording, its vectors, and its topic memberships and causes affected aggregate
evidence to be recalculated.

## Common failure modes

### The page has questions but no grounding rate

Those answers have not produced a grounding verdict. Demand is still real, but the
page marks the grounding state as unmeasured instead of treating it as healthy or
failed.

### A topic has no recommendation

The topic may have too little active evidence, corpus analysis may still be running,
or the corpus check may be unavailable. Open the topic to see the explicit evidence and
enrichment state.

### A shared topic link changes after opening

Closely related topics can merge. Radioso resolves a still-valid old identifier to the
surviving topic and replaces the URL. Expired or inaccessible identifiers return the
same not-found view.
