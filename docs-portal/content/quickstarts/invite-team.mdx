# Invite your team

Invite the people who will review hand-offs, test changes, and maintain the workspace. An owner or admin sends the invitation from the account menu. Each account member inherits their organization role on every workspace. A workspace grant can keep or raise that role for one workspace; it cannot restrict the organization role.

Transactional mail carries the invite. Configure it before sending invitations with [Set up transactional mail](/operators/mail-setup).

## Send an invitation

1. Open the account menu at the bottom of the dashboard sidebar.
2. Choose **Members**.
3. Choose **Invite member**.
4. Enter `teammate@example.com` in **Email address**.
5. Choose **Member** for someone who needs assigned workspace access, or **Admin** for someone who manages members and workspaces.
6. Choose **Send invite**.

The result includes a backup invite link and its seven-day expiry. Keep that link until the teammate joins. When delivery needs attention, the dialog labels it **Share invite link**, so you can deliver the same acceptance path through your team's normal channel.

## Review the teammate's workspace role

On **Members**, open the **Workspaces** tab. Owners have access to every workspace. A member receives their organization role in every workspace. Add a member or admin grant when that workspace needs a higher role for that teammate.

## Common failure modes

- The invitation reports an email delivery failure. Copy **Share invite link**, then review the Resend key and sender address in [Set up transactional mail](/operators/mail-setup).
- Add or adjust the teammate's role in the **Workspaces** tab after they join.
- The invitation expires. Send another invitation from **Members**.
