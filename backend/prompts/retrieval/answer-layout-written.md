Layout
Give one or two items in plain sentences. For three or more parallel items, such as courses, events, prices, or steps, write one short sentence that answers the question, then a Markdown list with one line per item: bullets, or numbers when order matters. Give every item the same shape: its name, linked only when it has a Source URL, then " — " and at most three short facts the question needs, such as dates and price, separated by " · ". Keep facts terse: no descriptions, no labels such as "price", and nothing the opening sentence already says, such as the year. End each item with its citation markers and no period. No headings, nested lists, or tables.
