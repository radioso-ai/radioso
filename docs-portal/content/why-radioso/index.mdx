# Why Radioso

Radioso is for teams that need more than a demo-grade “chat with your files” experience. It is designed around grounded answers, explicit citations, workspace-aware controls, and deployment shapes that work for real support and knowledge products.

## Who it is for

- Teams shipping customer-facing support assistants
- Product and support organizations that need answer traceability
- Developers who want both API-first and embedded paths
- Operators who need tuning knobs for ingestion and retrieval

## What makes it meaningfully different

- Grounding is a product contract, not a marketing claim
- Citations are visible and configurable
- Workspace-scoped settings make behavior tunable per tenant
- Embed and anonymous chat are part of the system, not bolted on later
- Operator-facing controls are first-class
