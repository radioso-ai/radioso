# Subprocessors on Radioso Cloud

When your agent talks to a visitor on Radioso Cloud, your organization is the controller of that conversation and Ljuv OÜ, the Estonian company that operates Radioso Cloud, is your processor. A few outside companies do part of that processing for Ljuv OÜ: they host the service, run the models, and send its mail. Those are the subprocessors, and this page lists them for each Radioso Cloud region with what each one receives and where it processes it.

The same list is Annex 3 of the data processing agreement. A self-hosted deployment has none of these unless its operator configures them; [Data residency](/operators/data-residency) covers the services a self-hosted deployment can turn on.

## Find your region

Radioso Cloud runs as two separate stacks. Each has its own database, document storage, task queues and secrets, and a workspace lives on exactly one of them. The address you sign in at tells you which:

| Stack | Dashboard | Hosting region |
|---|---|---|
| live-eu | `app.radioso.ai` | Google Cloud `europe-west1`, Belgium |
| live | `app-us.radioso.ai` | Google Cloud `us-central1`, Iowa, United States |

The two stacks use the same subprocessors. Where something differs between them, the tables say so.

## Subprocessors for every workspace

These four process personal data for every workspace on both stacks.

| Subprocessor | What it does | What it receives | Where, live-eu | Where, live |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud | Hosts Radioso: the application, the PostgreSQL database, document storage, task queues, secrets and logs | Everything the workspace stores: conversations, documents, visitor records, settings | `europe-west1`, Belgium. Request and application logs are held in Cloud Logging's `global` location. | `us-central1`, Iowa. Request and application logs are held in Cloud Logging's `global` location. |
| OpenAI | Runs Radioso's models: answers, Ray, query rewriting, reranking, conversation summaries, and the embeddings and metadata extraction that make documents searchable | For each turn, the visitor's message, the conversation so far, the document passages retrieved for it and the agent's instructions; the text of each document it processes; Ray conversations | OpenAI's global API | OpenAI's global API |
| Resend | Sends the mail Radioso sends | Each recipient's address and the message: sign-in verification, password resets and teammate invitations, and the contact requests and routine notices your agents send, which carry what the visitor wrote and the values a routine collected | United States | United States |
| PostHog | Collects Radioso's error reports and application logs, so the team running the service can find faults | Error messages, stack traces, identifiers, counts and outcomes. Before export, Radioso redacts fields named for message bodies, content, prompts, credentials and tokens. | EU (`eu.i.posthog.com`) | EU (`eu.i.posthog.com`) |

A few details matter in practice:

- **OpenAI applies when the workspace runs Radioso's models.** Every model call goes to OpenAI with Radioso's key unless the workspace stores its own key for that capability (see [your own accounts](#services-that-run-under-your-own-account)). Radioso's key reaches OpenAI's global API on both stacks. To keep model traffic for one workspace in the EU, bring a key from an OpenAI project created in Europe and pick **EU** next to it.
- **Resend sees visitor data only when an agent mails your team.** Account mail goes through Resend for every organization. A contact request or routine notice reaches Resend only when an agent or routine sends one to an email recipient you configured. The email channel, which also runs on Resend, is not offered on either Radioso Cloud stack.
- **Google Cloud's logs are held in Cloud Logging's `global` location on both stacks, for 30 days.** Request logs record the address and browser of each request. [Data retention](/operators/data-retention#backups-and-logs-on-radioso-cloud) lists how long backups and logs outlive a deletion.

## Services for your Radioso account

These handle data about your organization's own Radioso account: who signs in and who pays. Ljuv OÜ is the controller of that data under its privacy policy, so these services fall outside the data processing agreement. Neither receives conversation data.

| Service | What it does | What it receives | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stripe | Takes payment for paid plans and top-ups | The billing email, the organization's Radioso id, and the name, address, tax id and payment details entered at Stripe's checkout or billing portal. Radioso never sees a full card number. | United States |
| Google Sign-In | Signs a person in when they choose **Sign in with Google** | Google tells Radioso the person's email address and name | Google's global service |

## Services that run under your own account

Some features send data to a provider using credentials your workspace supplies. Radioso sends the data on your instruction, and your own agreement with that provider governs what it does with it, so these providers are yours rather than Ljuv OÜ's subprocessors. Each one applies only once you connect it.

- **Your own model provider key** — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, stored under **Settings → Providers**. A capability that selects a provider with a stored key uses that key in place of Radioso's. Radioso's own models on Radioso Cloud are OpenAI's, so Anthropic and Gemini only ever receive data through a workspace's own key. A workspace OpenAI key carries its own residency choice: Global, US, or EU. See [Data residency](/operators/data-residency#openai).
- **ElevenLabs, for voice** — a voice runtime key is your own ElevenLabs account. The call's audio, and its recording, live in that account, in the region the agent's **Voice** card picks. Radioso receives the transcript of each turn and answers it like any other message.
- **Slack** — once you install Radioso's Slack app, it reads the messages addressed to your agent and posts replies, and notices to the channels you pick, in your Slack workspace. Slack keeps them under your agreement with Slack.
- **WhatsApp** — the WhatsApp connector sends and receives through your own WhatsApp Business account on Meta's Cloud API.
- **Endpoints you name** — webhooks, external MCP servers, and the WordPress connector send to and read from the addresses you configure.

## How changes are announced

At least 30 days before Ljuv OÜ adds or replaces a subprocessor on a stack, it emails the owners of every organization on that stack and updates this page. The email names the company, what it will do and receive, and where it processes. You can object within those 30 days; the data processing agreement says what happens next. Changes to the services in the last section are yours to make: they start when you connect one and stop when you remove it.

## Common failure modes

- **Model traffic still reaches OpenAI's global API after you add your own key** — the residency choice next to a workspace OpenAI key starts at Global. Pick EU to match a key from a Europe-region OpenAI project.
- **A capability still runs on Radioso's models after you add a Claude or Gemini key** — a stored key takes over only for the capabilities that select its provider. Check the provider each job uses on the **Models** card under **Settings → Providers**, or with `GET /api/v1/settings/llm-models`.
- **Voice calls land at ElevenLabs in the United States** — the agent's Voice card starts at United States. Pick the region the ElevenLabs account is provisioned for; EU needs an Enterprise ElevenLabs account.
