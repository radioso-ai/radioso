# Grounded answers

Radioso is designed for answers that can be checked. That means retrieval quality matters, citations matter, and unsupported answers need explicit handling rather than vague confidence theater.

## Product stance

- Retrieval is tunable
- Citations are part of the answer experience
- Unsupported questions should degrade honestly
- Operators need visibility into why a response was produced
