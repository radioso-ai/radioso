# Amendment: Quick-Reply Lane

**Amends**: `spec.md` (FR-002, FR-003, FR-005, FR-006, SC-001) and the fused-planning rule that pre-retrieval planning never receives answer instructions
**Created**: 2026-10-13
**Status**: Approved

The turn plan gains three last fields, in this order: `requestPresent: boolean`, true when the latest message asks for, or asks about, anything beyond the greeting, thanks, farewell, or acknowledgement itself; `openQuestion: boolean`, true when the assistant's latest message asked the user to choose, confirm, or provide something or offered options; and `quickReply: { kind: "greeting" | "thanks" | "farewell" | "acknowledgement" | "none"; language; text }`, where `language` is the BCP 47 tag of the reply's language (null for `none`), so that on a direct turn of pure conversational mechanics the planner writes the reply in the same call and the turn makes one model call instead of two. These three fields are the single, deliberate exemption from FR-003's all-or-nothing parsing: a missing or non-boolean `requestPresent` or `openQuestion`, or a quick reply that is missing, malformed, of an unknown kind, blank, longer than `CHAT_BEHAVIOR.turnPlanning.maxQuickReplyCharacters` (320) after trimming, or without a BCP 47 `language` tag (missing, null, or a name such as "English"), reads as `kind: "none"` with the unreadable signal set to the value that keeps the lane closed, the rest of the plan stays valid and drives routing, routines, directives, and language as before, and the plan records `quickReplyDiscarded` for the trace. Every other field keeps whole-plan rejection. The planner follows standing instructions when it writes a reply: the agent's custom instruction first, then the direct route's global, repeatable, always-on answer directives, rendered in its stable system prefix under "Standing Instructions For Any Reply You Write". This is the recorded exception to "planning never receives answer instructions": the custom instruction reaches the planner only inside that block, which states that the instructions govern the quickReply text alone and never the route, rewrite, response language, routine rankings, or directive classifications. A standing instruction is operator-authored truth, so a quick reply may repeat a fact one supplies word for word and states no other. When the standing instructions alone would exceed `maxEstimatedPromptTokens`, the turn plans without them and the lane is off for that turn. The host, not the planner, decides whether the reply is sent (`backend/src/modules/chat/services/turnPlanQuickReply.ts`): only on a resolved plan (bypassed and failed plans keep FR-006's staged behavior, so active, parked, or claiming routines, pending clarifications, and over-budget prompts never use it, and a human-owned conversation never reaches answer selection), when both the plan and the interpreted turn are direct, the kind is not `none`, `requestPresent` and `openQuestion` are false, the plan wrote no retrieval rewrite whichever route it chose, the turn is not an identity question, no routine yielded the turn, and the turn's resolved answer steering is exactly the always-on directives the planner wrote under. Any conditional directive that applies to the answer, or any always-on directive the runtime held back, sends the turn to the composer unchanged. The words come in two layers. When the agent has authored `mechanicsReplies` for the kind in the reply's language (the planner names it as a BCP 47 tag; lookup is exactly that tag, then its primary subtag, case-insensitive, and uses the planner's tag alone, so a portable configuration speaks the same line on every channel whatever its page, browser, or default locale), the turn speaks an authored line chosen by rotation and drops the planner's text: authored lines give the guarantee. Otherwise the planner's text goes out only if it carries no digit, currency symbol, URL, or email address. That gate is structural and catches prices, numbers, and links; it is not a guarantee against every invented fact. SC-001 reads accordingly: an eligible direct turn makes two model calls, or one when its quick reply is used.
