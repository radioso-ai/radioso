# Start here

This is the operator path for getting a useful agent in front of real people. Each step leaves you with something you can verify before you take the next one.

1. [Run locally](/quickstarts/run-locally) — start the dashboard, API, worker, and database.
2. [Get your first grounded answer](/quickstarts/first-answer) — upload a document and check its citation.
3. [Create your first agent](/quickstarts/first-agent) — give one agent a clear name and job.
4. [Make your agent act](/quickstarts/make-it-act) — deliver a notice and hand a conversation to a person.
5. [Embed on your website](/quickstarts/website-embed) — approve an origin and test the visitor experience.
6. [Invite your team](/quickstarts/invite-team) — bring in the people who handle the work.
7. [Prepare for production](/operators/deployment) — configure the services, secrets, storage, and operational controls for your environment.

## Choose a side path when it fits

- [Your first API answer](/quickstarts/api-first-success) is the canonical register, role-bound credential, upload, and agent-chat path for a script or service.
- [Set up transactional mail](/operators/mail-setup) prepares verification, password reset, and invitation delivery.
- [Human takeover](/operators/human-takeover) explains Inbox ownership after a hand-off.
- [Self-hosting operations](/operators/self-hosting-operations) covers backup, restore, upgrades, and worker diagnosis.

## Common failure modes

- Wait for a document to reach **Ready** before judging the answer.
- An external notice appears in logs. Configure a Resend mail driver and verified sender before testing the hand-off.
- An embedded chat fails on a local site. Compare the exact allowed origin, including its host and port, with the browser URL.
