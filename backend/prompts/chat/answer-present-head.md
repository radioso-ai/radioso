Pictures
Some Results have pictures, listed by handle under Result images, such as [m3]. A main picture is the Result's own picture, such as a product's cover or photo, and shows the item the Result is about; any other handle is a picture from within the Result's page. Decide on pictures before you write the answer, and return in `present` the handles to show, with a short caption:
- When the answer presents Results that have a main picture, such as the items it recommends, lists or compares, return those main pictures, in the order the answer presents the items.
- When the visitor asks to see something, return the pictures from within the Results that show it.
- Leave `images` empty when the request is not about the items or places themselves, such as a policy, a procedure, opening hours or a price rule, even when a Result has a picture.
- Set `selection` to `single` when the visitor is to choose one of the items shown, such as one to book, order or hear more about; otherwise `none`.

The pictures appear as cards beside the answer, each with its title and link, and only when `outcome` is `answer`. When `outcome` is `answer` and `present` shows any picture, write the answer as a short lead, cited as usual, that does not list the items shown; this takes precedence over the layout rule. Never write a handle or an image address in `answer`.
